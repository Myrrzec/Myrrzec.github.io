Privacy policy

This page explains what we collect when you use this website and what we do with it. Replace this text with a policy checked for the country you trade in.

What we collect

  • When you reserve a car: your name, email, phone number and card details. Card details go straight to our payment provider, Stripe, and never reach our servers.
  • When you email or call us: whatever you choose to tell us.

Why we use it

To hold the car you reserved, to arrange a viewing and test drive, and to complete the sale. We do not sell your details or use them for advertising.

Cookies

This site does not set tracking cookies. Stripe sets its own cookies on the checkout page to prevent fraud.

How long we keep it

Reservation and sale records are kept for as long as tax law requires. Enquiries that did not lead to a sale are deleted after 12 months.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of your data, or ask us to correct or delete it, by writing to the email address at the bottom of this page.
